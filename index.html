About RAAA Rubber

Built on experience.
Driven by improvement.

RAAA Rubber Industries was founded in 2009 under the dynamic leadership of Mr. S. Rajkumar. In our company, we had installed machinery from Asian countries to ensure faster and efficient production, reduction of costs and as a result it has reduced manpower. Our company had the great steady growth to achieve milestone.

Since our establishment, RAAA Rubber Industries has been at the forefront of revolutionizing the rubber manufacturing landscape. With a relentless commitment to excellence and innovation, we have consistently delivered unparalleled quality products to our customers.
